Tradition, heritage & values

The story of Metal Prod Service

True precision is not just about programmable machines; it is about the dedication, character, and honesty of the people who operate them.

Metal Prod Service was established with a foundational mission: to provide the European and domestic mechanical industries with uncompromising precision parts manufactured with familial care, punctuality, and competitive pricing.

Over decades of generational industrial experience, we expanded from basic mechanical workshops into a high-technology CNC machining and thermal treatment facility. As an independent, family-owned business, we do not view clients as simple order numbers. Every project — from a single custom prototype shaft for a broken production line to 50,000 serialized precision screws — receives personal oversight from our senior engineering team.

Uncompromising seriousness & integrity

When we quote a delivery date, we meet it. When we guarantee an H7 tolerance or 60 HRC surface hardness, every unit is certified.

Direct engineer-to-engineer dialogue

No layered corporate bureaucracy. Clients speak directly with tooling and machining specialists who understand CAD models, thermal expansion, and mechanical stresses.

Fair market pricing

Family-owned financial agility means zero inflated overheads. We pass operational efficiencies directly to our clients.

Dedication to perfection

We inspect the first part, the middle part, and the final run with equal rigor using calibrated micrometric gauges and CMM arms.
